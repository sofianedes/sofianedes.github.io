Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This website and the small publishing tool linked to it ("the App") are operated by Sophiane Bell.

Data collected

This website collects no personal data: no account, no form, no cookies, no analytics.

The App is used only by its owner, on his own computer, to publish his own videos to his own TikTok account. When he authorizes it, TikTok provides an access token and basic account information (such as the account name). This information is used only to upload and publish his videos, is stored only on his own computer, and is never sold, shared or sent to any third party.

Other users

The App does not access, collect or store data about any other TikTok user.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings ("Security and permissions" › "Manage app permissions"); the stored token is then deleted from the owner's computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message @aipocalypse13 on TikTok.
